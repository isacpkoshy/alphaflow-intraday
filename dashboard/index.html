      Stage Transition Reference

      • Stage 1 (Neglect / Base): Moving averages flat/entangled. Do not lock up capital.
      • Stage 2 (Advancing / Markup): 50-SMA > 150-SMA > 200-SMA, price above all SMAs, RS ≥ 75. AI HOLD.
      • Stage 3 (Top / Distribution): Churn at highs, volume expansion on down days. Breakeven / target ratchet armed.
      • Stage 4 (Decline / Capitulation): Price below 50 & 200 SMA. Immediate AI EXIT.

      Trailing Stop Enforcement

      • Minervini Rule: Never allow a profit of 3R to turn into a loss. Lock breakeven at +2R.
      • Afzal Rule: Trail along the rising 50-day SMA or 10-day EMA depending on volatility.
      • Failed Breakout Rule: Any stock falling >4% below pivot triggers immediate risk cut.
